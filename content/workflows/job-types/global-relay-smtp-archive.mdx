# Global Relay SMTP Archive

Archive email through Global Relay SMTP for compliance.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | Finance and Accounting |
| **Where you pick it** | Step 1: Choose Your Job Type |

## How it works

1. Configure Global Relay settings.
2. Send/archive messages.
3. Keep for record-keeping workflows.

## In a workflow tree

- Add as a child under a **Group** (or under another job).

- Add children for the next step after this job finishes.
