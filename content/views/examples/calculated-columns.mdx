# Calculated columns

**Goal:** Derive **MarketValue** and **PnL** from quantity, price, and cost using calculated select columns.

This stays in **all matching rows** mode — one output row per lot.

## Sample data

[`lots_cost.csv`](/examples/lots_cost.csv) — 8 rows, 5 columns

| LotId | Security | Quantity | Price | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | AAPL | 100 | 185.50 | 18000 |
| L2 | MSFT | 50 | 390.00 | 18500 |
| L3 | GOOG | 25 | 150.00 | 4000 |
| L4 | TSLA | 10 | 200.00 | 2500 |
| L5 | AMZN | 40 | 175.00 | 6000 |
| L6 | META | 15 | 480.00 | 7500 |
| L7 | NVDA | 80 | 500.00 | 32000 |
| L8 | NFLX | 5 | 450.00 | 2000 |

## Build the view

1. **Create View**
2. **Table** = `lots_cost`
3. **I want to** = all matching rows
4. **Select columns**
   - Reference: LotId, Security, Quantity, Price, Cost
   - **Calculated**, display name `MarketValue`:
     - Quantity `*` Price
   - **Calculated**, display name `PnL`:
     - MarketValue is not available as a prior select column in the expression builder — build it as Quantity `*` Price `-` Cost  
       (or nest a group: `(Quantity * Price) - Cost`)
5. **Save**

### Expected result

| LotId | Security | Quantity | Price | Cost | MarketValue | PnL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | AAPL | 100 | 185.50 | 18000 | 18550 | 550 |
| L2 | MSFT | 50 | 390.00 | 18500 | 19500 | 1000 |
| L3 | GOOG | 25 | 150.00 | 4000 | 3750 | -250 |
| L4 | TSLA | 10 | 200.00 | 2500 | 2000 | -500 |
| L5 | AMZN | 40 | 175.00 | 6000 | 7000 | 1000 |
| L6 | META | 15 | 480.00 | 7500 | 7200 | -300 |
| L7 | NVDA | 80 | 500.00 | 32000 | 40000 | 8000 |
| L8 | NFLX | 5 | 450.00 | 2000 | 2250 | 250 |

Winners: NVDA (+8000), MSFT / AMZN (+1000). Losers: TSLA (−500), META (−300), GOOG (−250).

## Optional: only profitable lots

In **Where conditions**, after you have PnL (or filter on the same expression):

- `PnL` `>` `0`

That keeps L1, L2, L5, L7, L8.
