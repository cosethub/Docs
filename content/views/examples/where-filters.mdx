# Where filters

**Goal:** Keep only **Active** positions for a single as-of date, using where conditions (and a nested AND group).

## Sample data

[`active_positions.csv`](/examples/active_positions.csv) — 8 rows, 6 columns

| AsOfDate | Account | Security | Status | Quantity | MarketValue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC1 | AAPL | Active | 100 | 18500 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC1 | MSFT | Active | 50 | 19500 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC1 | GOOG | Closed | 25 | 3750 |
| 2024-01-30 | ACC2 | AAPL | Active | 200 | 36800 |
| 2024-01-30 | ACC2 | TSLA | Pending | 10 | 2000 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC2 | MSFT | Active | 75 | 29250 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC3 | AMZN | Active | 15 | 2625 |
| 2024-01-29 | ACC3 | IBM | Closed | 20 | 3300 |

Without filters you get everything — including Closed / Pending and older dates.

## Build the view

1. **Create View**
2. **Table** = `active_positions`
3. **I want to** = all matching rows
4. **Select columns** — Reference: AsOfDate, Account, Security, Status, Quantity, MarketValue
5. **Where conditions** — add a **Group** with logical operator **AND**:
   - Condition: `Status` `=` `Active`
   - Condition: `AsOfDate` **between** `2024-01-31` and `2024-01-31`  
     (or equality on AsOfDate if your picker allows a single day)
6. **Save**

### Expected result

| AsOfDate | Account | Security | Status | Quantity | MarketValue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC1 | AAPL | Active | 100 | 18500 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC1 | MSFT | Active | 50 | 19500 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC2 | MSFT | Active | 75 | 29250 |
| 2024-01-31 | ACC3 | AMZN | Active | 15 | 2625 |

Dropped: GOOG (Closed), ACC2 AAPL (wrong date), TSLA (Pending), IBM (Closed + older date).

## Optional: text contains

To keep only Apple-related securities regardless of status:

- `Security` **contains** `AAPL`

That returns the two AAPL rows (Active on 01-31 and Active on 01-30).

## Optional: OR group

To include Active **or** Pending on 2024-01-31:

1. Outer group: `AsOfDate` between 2024-01-31 and 2024-01-31 (**AND**)
2. Nested group (**OR**):
   - `Status` `=` `Active`
   - `Status` `=` `Pending`

With this sample, Pending has no 01-31 row, so the result matches the Active-only table above.
